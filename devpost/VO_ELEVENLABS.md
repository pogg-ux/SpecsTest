# Voiceover lines for ElevenLabs — FaultLens

How to use:
1. Paste each clip into ElevenLabs separately and download as MP3 (name it VO01.mp3, VO02.mp3 ...).
2. Record one silent OBS take per clip, matching the "Screen" line, a little longer than the audio.
3. In Clipchamp, put each video take on the timeline with its VO clip on top. Trim video to fit.
Spellings below are written for speech on purpose (e.g. "T S four", "F one"). Do not "fix" them.
Target total: about 2 minutes 40 seconds.

---

**VO01 — Cold open (~8 s)**
Screen: Step card with trace showing; enter 0.0 at F1 OUT; F1 turns red; "F1 open".
> The actuator won't extend. My glasses just found the open fuse. And it never guessed.

**VO02 — The problem (~14 s)**
Screen: Slow pull back over the cabinet.
> I'm an industrial machine service tech. On a call, I'm juggling a meter, a printed schematic, and a manual, with my eyes off the machine every few seconds. This puts the schematic on the machine.

**VO03 — Start and Library (~14 s)**
Screen: Reset. Safety notice, Dismiss. Library menu upper left.
> It starts with a safety reminder. Then the Library menu: job, job control, report, camera, notes, and options. It folds away when I don't need it. The job files for this machine are already loaded: the wiring, and the tolerance for every test point.

**VO04 — Symptom to path (~22 s)**
Screen: Job, Report symptom, "EXTEND doesn't work". Outlines and trace appear. Zoom on step card.
> I report the symptom. It reasons from the schematic. Retract works, so the power supply and the switch are good. The extend path is wire two A, terminal T S four, wire two B, fuse F one, wire two C, and lamp L one. It outlines those parts and traces the wire run right on the panel.

**VO05 — Schematic (~10 s)**
Screen: Tap Schematic. Zoomed, labeled section with wire 2b highlighted.
> If I want the print, it shows just the section I need, with the wire I'm chasing highlighted.

**VO06 — Readings (~24 s)**
Screen: Enter 12.1 at TS-4 (green), 12.0 at F1 IN (green), 0.0 at F1 OUT (red).
> I enter what my meter actually reads. Every test point has its own tolerance from the job file. For these lamps, that's twelve volts, plus or minus ten percent. Twelve point one at T S four. Good. Twelve at the fuse input. Good. Zero at the fuse output.

**VO07 — Finding (~14 s)**
Screen: Finding card. Zoom on "F1 open" and the next action.
> F one is open. It tells me exactly what to do. Lock out, check continuity, and replace it with the same half amp fast blow fuse. Don't upsize.

**VO08 — No guess (~12 s)**
Screen: Enter 6.0 at F1 OUT. Amber message.
> And if a reading doesn't fit anything in the files, like six volts here, it doesn't make something up. It tells me to recheck my leads.

**VO09 — Retest, notes, report (~18 s)**
Screen: Fuse replaced, retest. S1 UP. L1 lights. Enter 12.0, green. Notes, add "Fuse replaced". Library, Job control, End job, Yes. Job report: open the filed job under Previous jobs; flip to Notes and pictures tab.
> Retest. The lamp is on, and the reading is logged. I add a note, take a snapshot, and end the job. It files the report, with every reading, note, picture, and time, and leaves a clean board for the next job.

**VO10 — Hide (~10 s)**
Screen: Hide cards; labels stay on parts. Then Show.
> When I need to work, one tap clears the cards, and keeps the labels on the parts.

**VO11 — Close (~12 s)**
Screen: Full view of the cabinet with labels.
> It's rule based, so it never invents an answer. Next up: live part recognition, manual lookup, and job history. Fault Lens, the hands-free AR field assistant.

---

ElevenLabs tips
- Pick one voice and keep it for every clip. Calm, clear, mid-pace.
- Stability around 50–60% keeps it steady across clips.
- If a part name sounds off, adjust the spelling (e.g. "F-one") and regenerate just that clip.
- Leave about half a second of silence between clips on the timeline.
